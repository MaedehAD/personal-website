Research

Research Vision

By harnessing digital technologies and especifically AI-powered systems, I would like to invetigate ways to help individuals to stay healthier. Inspiring from my background I would like to bring together education, behavior change, and adaptive human-centered technologies to improve people's health literacy, assist them to acquire self-efficay on taking care of their well-being, get into healthy habits and use AI to adhere on the lifelong wellness.

Research Pillars

I am interested in:
Educate
Promoting health literacy to help people understand preventive behaviors, healthy habits, and why persistence matters. In addition, facilitating self-efficacy improvement of patient on their care path.
Engage
Using change-management strategies to help individuals stay engaged and adhere to healthy behaviors or prescribed rehabilitations.
Understand
Investigate users' information needs, collect related data, and visualize them as an interpretable way through explainable, human-centered systems.
Sustain
Developing AI-based adaptive support systems for healthy habits and scaffolding systems for patients' long-term care.

Methodological Expertise

Qualitative Research: I was involved in collecting interview data, thematic analysis, theory development through NVivo.

Quantitative Research: I have developed surveys, collected data via them and analyzed data through structural equation modeling and statistical analysis.

Human-centered Design: I ran a usability study to evalaute mobile and wearable technology through co-design, and iterative collaboration with developers and users.

Intelligent Health Systems: I am working on predictive modeling, explainable AI and federated learning of health data.

Selected qualitative research

African American women in computing. I analyzed interview data in NVivo and contributed to theory development examining familial influences and persistence in the computing pipeline.

Motivation to engage in aging research. As part of the NIH-funded Adherence Promotion with Person-centered Technology (APPT) project, I analyzed qualitative survey and contributed to theory development around older adults’ motivations to participate in research.
